MATH 100 Elements of Finite Mathematics and Calculus
Fall 2026
Grading: 10% quizzes, 25% each midterm, and 40% final.
Midterm 1 will be held in week 6 and Midterm 2 in week 11. Both exams are closed-book.
The final exam covers all topics. Date, time and location will be announced later.
There will be six 10-minute quizzes during problem sessions. The highest four quizzes will be counted and the worst two quizzes will be dropped.
Attendance will be taken on random lecture dates and is worth up to 5 bonus points.
Make-up exam: one common exam at the end of the semester, only with a valid medical report.
