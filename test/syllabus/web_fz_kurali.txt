Office hours: by appointment.
Grading
Homeworks: 20%
Midterm: 40%
Final: 40%
To be eligible to take the final exam, a student must earn at least 30% of each of the first three homework grades and at least 30% on the midterm. Otherwise the student receives an FZ grade.
Attendance is recorded and reported to the department, but it has no direct effect on grades or FZ status.
Any code that fails to compile will receive a zero grade.
